# Paddy Lane destination

A short eastward branch from the laterite loop reaches a fictional neighbourhood
at (285, 110). Three homes have different sizes/colours, tiled roofs, verandas and
small planted yards. Six plots use terrain-following surfaces, instanced rice,
bunds and narrow irrigation channels. A produce stall sits beside the lane.

Eight residents follow the saved world clock: two field workers, a vendor, two
staggered shoppers and three children. They move to local work/play positions and
return to covered home approaches in rain or after their activity window. Children
move around a ball in a railed courtyard; the jeep footprint excludes that area.
The stall's twelve portions decrease on unique shopper visits and reset each day;
stock and positions are saved. A witnessed field-work visit can create a memory.

This is a small playable destination, not a complete agricultural economy. Crop
seasons, harvest/delivery chains, player purchases, home interiors and sophisticated
children's game rules remain future work. Existing scenery and simulation helpers
handle shared materials, instance batching and resource disposal. Malayalam/local
architectural review and phone visual review are left on KADAL_PLAYTEST.md.

## Market junction and scenery follow-up

The lane continues to a bakery and cycle-repair shop, with staff-driven shutters,
a local pedestrian and marked jeep parking. A morning van run uses saved position,
phase and unloading time, waits for people/the parked jeep, pauses unloading in
rain and replenishes the produce stall once per daily run. Shops are exterior
activity scenes; player shopping and detailed stock for bakery/repairs are future
work. Field borders gain planted detail, home gardens gain walls/clotheslines,
and morning/evening sunlight has a warmer tint.
