# Kerala photo-story pilot

Three optional physical photo frames connect fictional Kadal with real Kerala:
shore boats, coir spinning in Kollam, and a tea shop at Thattekkaad. Small local
textures load within 45 world units; large local photographs load only in the
reader. All six image variants total under 1 MB. Missing images retain readable
text and a source link. Textures are disposed when the world closes, including
late image completions.

Inspecting opens the existing accessible dialog and pauses the world. Each story
includes a real/fiction distinction, caption, date, credit, licence, sources,
English and draft Malayalam. Keeping records a stable story ID in the existing
memory/passport system; the portal receives its normal memory summary. Images
are never placed in localStorage. Reopening a saved story needs no return journey.

The tea story includes an original fictional film-club mood experiment. It has
no actor portraits, movie stills, copied film dialogue, scores or required task.
The story registry lives in src/game/stories.js; media permissions and original
sources are in public/stories/CREDITS.md. Metadata was checked against the source
pages; independent local/editorial and Malayalam review are still outstanding.

Creative input requested: preferred visual tone, real locations and personal
stories, and Malayalam films the user wants considered. User-owned photographs
can replace the pilot selection when supplied with location, creator credit,
and permission for game use. Do not infer publication permission from a photo
being sent as a visual reference. No deployment was performed for this pilot.

## Tea-shop life follow-up

The existing shop now serves present residents, with a four-second tea pour,
finite snack portions per morning/afternoon batch and saved service state.
Leela keeps the shop open during the midday break; rain can interrupt service.
The fictional Kadal menu alternates pazham pori and parippuvada. Regional menu
profiles and local review remain future work; this does not represent all Kerala.

An in-world TV shows original lightweight animated football at midday and a
rainy-road short in late afternoon. Jaya/Rajan watch during bounded breaks and
face the screen; existing routes, rain shelter and ferry logic retain priority.
Short original tones, a pouring cue, nearby text and contextual resident lines
support noticing. There is no recorded speech, licensed broadcast or new menu.

Serving is automatic for nearby resident visits; player snack purchases and
physical customer cup handoffs are not implemented. Faith spaces, sevens matches,
and historical story releases remain subsequent roadmap batches.
