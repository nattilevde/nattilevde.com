Reviewed live https://nattilevde.com in the in-app browser at its existing approximately 831 × 837 viewport. No application code changed. Opening Alappuzha updated the browser-local district discovery state automatically.

1. Home: strong green/cream identity, photography and typography. Map heading overlaps northern district labels at this viewport; floating guide competes with lower map controls. Give the map its own area, move explanatory content outside it and collapse the sidebar earlier. Small map labels and control text warrant readability and target-size testing.
2. Alappuzha district: readable and coherent, but the inspected district offers only two place entries. Expand depth with food, people, everyday life and historical stories, supported by credited images and sources.
3. Alleppey Backwaters detail: attractive but brief, consisting primarily of a short description and tip. Add uniqueness, local voice, source links and related discoveries. District and place both retain the root URL; introduce shareable URLs and browser-back navigation. Opening a district marks it visited, so distinguish reading, saving and in-game discovery in progress labels.

Recommended sequence: fix map layout/readability; deepen three representative district/place stories; connect relevant stories to supported locations in the game. Use labels that distinguish real-world facts from fictional inspired locations. Later, add editorial themed trails and optional Malayalam/local audio. Do not treat existing generic game entry as a supported direct location jump.
