# Highland drive

The new ridge road starts at the east end of Paddy Lane (381, 110), winds through the existing rising terrain, and connects to the original hill road at (690, -325). It creates a return loop through the foothills rather than a dead-end destination. World bounds and existing saved positions remain unchanged.

A narrower laterite estate track branches at (448, 140), passes a discoverable cultivated bend, and rejoins at (555, 85). The ridge spur at (705, 90) leads to parking, a discovery and a resting bench near (731, 136). These are fictional places. Woodland uses shared instanced foliage; reflector posts mark the climbing road. Existing terrain-based jeep suspension, slope forces, rain traction and lights apply.

This batch adds scenery and connected driving, not an estate economy or new residents. Edge posts are visual markers, not collision barriers. The road is not a simulation of dangerous cliffs; manual steering, downhill braking, visual terrain alignment and phone performance still need playtesting. Curved road footprint and grade checks supplement a browser render check and existing jeep regressions.
