# Kerala world: next cultural evolution

Principle: WORLD → LIFE → CURIOSITY → EXPLORATION → DISCOVERY → MEMORY.
Each addition must have a place, people, conditions, a distant cue and a reason to revisit.
Participation stays optional. Weather and local routines drive availability, not a quest checklist.

## Order of implementation

1. **Sevens after dark — first slice implemented.** Existing possession, dribbling,
   shots and goalkeeper simulation continues until approximately 21:30. Two gradual,
   shadow-free floodlights illuminate the ground from dusk through departure, with
   pitch-side benches. Rain still interrupts play. Next slice: spectators who arrive,
   shelter and leave, then score-driven reactions. Malayalam PA needs a consenting
   speaker and an original recording. Do not fill stands with motionless people.
2. **Fireflies and night photography.** Small inland pockets responding to time and
   weather, with restrained particle counts. Existing postcards make a quiet reward.
   Coastal bioluminescence follows as a rare, researched condition, not a nightly effect.
3. **Chenda practice.** Expand the existing rhythm interaction into learnable patterns,
   a practising musician and an optional response. Distinguish tayambaka, melam and
   panchavadyam; obtain performer-reviewed patterns and licensed recordings before
   presenting a named performance tradition.
4. **Vallam kali training.** First build a suitable lake reach and a small practice
   crew whose cadence visibly affects motion. Full snake boats need dedicated space,
   synchronised animation and performance testing; do not squeeze them into a canal.
   Authentic vanchipattu needs credited, licensed singing. A race comes after the
   water, crew, spectators and transport work independently.
5. **Onam preparations.** Pookalam gradually changes, kitchens prepare sadya and
   neighbours gather. Persistent festival progression should allow missed days without
   penalties. Pulikali needs reviewed visual and movement references before expansion.
6. **Seasonal return visits.** Wet field edges, fuller waterways, changing work and
   soundscapes before stronger rain. Preserve long dry intervals and short showers.
   Do not turn real disasters into entertainment or make roads routinely unusable.
7. **Ghat scooter and photography.** Reuse tested vehicle/camera foundations; add
   distinct handling only after road readability and safe stopping places improve.
   Reward framing and remembered places rather than mandatory score chasing.
8. **Specialist practices and large gatherings.** Kalari begins with a mentor and
   movement lesson, informed by a practitioner, before weapons or combat. Present it
   as a regional living practice; avoid unsupported origin or exclusivity claims.
   Toddy tapping begins as an observed working routine, with expert references before
   a climbing interaction. Kettukazhcha needs correct scale, routes and community
   references. Fireworks should be distant, optional, and respect reduced-flash and
   audio settings before any large display.
