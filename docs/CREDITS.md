## Audio

Every sound in the 3D world is synthesised at runtime from noise buffers and
oscillators in `src/game/audio.js`. There are no audio files, samples or
recordings in this project, so nothing here carries a third-party audio licence.

## World content

The 3D world is fictional. Place names such as Kadal Village, Kadalkotta Fort
and the Elavara Tea Estate are invented, and are inspired by — not
reconstructions of — real locations. Descriptive text about Kerala's culture,
food and landscape was written for this project.

the website itself does not use them. Moving them to `docs/` or `.github/` would
keep them out of the deployed site; the README paths would need updating to
match.

## Open questions

- **Provenance of the brand artwork.** How the logo and favicon were produced —
  drawn, commissioned, or generated with an AI tool — is not recorded here. It
  should be, along with the licence they are offered under, since the MIT
  licence on the source code is not automatically the right fit for artwork.
- **Unsplash per-photo attribution.** The Unsplash License does not require
  attribution, but it is requested, and the app currently credits Unsplash only
  as a source. Recording photographer names per image would be better practice.
- **Hotlinking.** Photographs are loaded directly from `images.unsplash.com` and
  `upload.wikimedia.org`. Wikimedia asks that reusers not hotlink at scale. If
  this project gets meaningful traffic, host or proxy the images instead.
